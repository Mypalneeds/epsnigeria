Our story

Emel Project Solutions was founded in Lagos in 2009 and is today the exclusive SSI SCHÄFER partner in Nigeria.

We began as a small trading company. Clients kept asking us to fit what we sold, so we built installation crews. Then they asked for warehouse racking, and we took on SSI SCHÄFER. The company now holds SEMA certification and has completed 500+ projects.
