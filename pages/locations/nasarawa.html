Warehouse racking and storage systems in Nasarawa

Emel Project Solutions supplies and installs warehouse racking, shelving and mezzanine floors in Nasarawa, with a free site visit.

Emel Project Solutions (EPS) supplies and installs warehouse racking and storage systems in Nasarawa. We are the exclusive SSI SCHÄFER partner in Nigeria and carry out SEMA-certified rack inspections in Lafia and Keffi.

North-central Nigeria connects the north and the south. Abuja drives commercial growth, and the states around it carry freight, distribution and agro-processing.

In Nasarawa, our work centres on warehousing and distribution growth next to Abuja, around Keffi and Karu.

We have delivered across the region from our Lagos base. For new warehouses in the FCT and neighbouring states we coordinate with your main contractor on programme, so racking installation fits the build sequence.

Heat in the dry season and heavy rain in the wet season both affect site work. We plan installation windows around them and specify finishes that hold colour in strong sun.
