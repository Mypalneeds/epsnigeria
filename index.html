How we work

From first call to handover, a project runs in ten steps. The first visit costs you nothing.

  1. Enquiry and brief

    You call, WhatsApp or fill the form. We ask what you store, the site location and your deadline.

  2. Site visit

    An EPS engineer visits, measures the building and checks floor and access. The visit is free.

  3. Design and layout

    We draw the rack layout around your pallets and forklifts and agree it with you.

  4. Quotation

    You get a written, no-obligation quote with scope, materials and programme.

  5. Approval and order

    You approve the drawing and quote. We place the order and plan delivery.

  6. Supply

    Racking comes from SSI SCHÄFER or our wider supplier network.

  7. Delivery to site

    We deliver and stage materials safely at your site, in any state.

  8. Installation

    Our crews install to the approved drawing, with safety checks as they go.

  9. Inspection and handover

    We check the finished work, test, and hand over load notices and documents.

  10. After-sales support

    Planned inspections, maintenance and spare parts when you need them.
